## 🎯 Course Objectives

1. Understand foundational concepts of formal languages, grammars, and abstract automata.
2. Design Deterministic and Non-deterministic Finite Automata (DFA, NFA) and convert between representations.
3. Analyze regular expressions, regular languages, and apply the Pumping Lemma for regular languages.
4. Design Context-Free Grammars (CFG) and Pushdown Automata (PDA) for context-free languages.
5. Understand Turing Machines, computability theory, decidability, and the Halting Problem.

## 📚 Module-Wise Syllabus

### Module 1: Finite Automata & Regular Languages
- Central concepts of Automata Theory: Alphabets, Strings, Languages, Empty string, Operations on languages
- Deterministic Finite Automata (DFA): Formal definition, Transition diagram, Transition table, Language of a DFA
- Non-Deterministic Finite Automata (NFA): Formal definition, Extended transition function
- Equivalence of DFA and NFA: Subset Construction algorithm
- NFA with Epsilon-transitions (NFA-ε): Epsilon-closures, Conversion of NFA-ε to DFA
- Minimization of DFA: Table Filling algorithm (Myhill-Nerode theorem basis)

### Module 2: Regular Expressions & Properties of Regular Languages
- Regular Expressions (RE): Operators, Precedence, Building regular expressions for formal languages
- Equivalence of Finite Automata and Regular Expressions: Conversion of RE to NFA-ε (Thompson's Construction)
- Conversion of DFA to Regular Expression (State Elimination method)
- Pumping Lemma for Regular Languages: Statement, Proof technique, Proving non-regularity of languages
- Closure Properties of Regular Languages: Union, Concatenation, Kleene star, Intersection, Complement, Reversal
- Decision Properties of Regular Languages: Emptiness, Finiteness, Membership, Equivalence

### Module 3: Context-Free Grammars (CFG) & Languages (CFL)
- Context-Free Grammars: Formal definition, Derivations (Leftmost and Rightmost derivations)
- Parse Trees (Derivation Trees): Relationship between parse trees and derivations
- Ambiguity in Grammars and Languages, Inherently ambiguous languages
- Simplification of Grammars: Elimination of Useless symbols, Epsilon-productions (Null), Unit-productions
- Normal Forms for CFG: Chomsky Normal Form (CNF), Greibach Normal Form (GNF) overview
- Pumping Lemma for Context-Free Languages and applications to show non-CFLs

### Module 4: Pushdown Automata (PDA)
- Pushdown Automata: Informal introduction, Formal definition, Transition function
- Instantaneous Descriptions (ID) of a PDA
- Language of a PDA: Acceptance by Final State, Acceptance by Empty Stack
- Equivalence of Acceptance by Final State and Empty Stack
- Equivalence of PDA and CFG: Construction of PDA from CFG and CFG from PDA
- Deterministic Pushdown Automata (DPDA): Definition, DPDA and regular languages, Parsing significance

### Module 5: Turing Machines & Decidability
- Turing Machine (TM): Formal definition, Instantaneous descriptions, Transition diagram
- Language acceptance by TM, Techniques for Turing Machine construction (Storage in state, Multiple tracks)
- Modifications of TM: Multitape TMs, Non-deterministic TMs, Equivalence with standard TM
- Computable Functions: Turing machines as transducers
- Decidability and Undecidability: Decidable problems, Recursively Enumerable (RE) and Recursive (R) languages
- The Halting Problem of Turing Machine, Reducibility
- Post Correspondence Problem (PCP), Introduction to Chomsky hierarchy of languages
