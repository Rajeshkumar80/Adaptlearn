## 🎯 Course Objectives

1. Recognize major components of the environment and ecological concepts.
2. Understand natural resources, their depletion, conservation, and sustainable development.
3. Study causes, effects, and control of environmental pollution (air, water, soil, noise, e-waste).
4. Learn environmental protection acts, policies, and international climate agreements.
5. Explore human population impacts, disaster management, and environmental ethics.

## 📚 Module-Wise Syllabus

### Module 1: Ecosystems & Biodiversity
- Definition, Scope, and Importance of Environmental Studies; Need for public awareness
- Ecosystem Concept: Structure and Function of an Ecosystem (Producers, Consumers, Decomposers)
- Energy Flow in Ecosystem: Food chains, Food webs, Ecological Pyramids
- Types of Ecosystems: Forest, Grassland, Desert, Aquatic ecosystems (Ponds, Rivers, Oceans)
- Biodiversity: Genetic, Species, and Ecosystem diversity; Hotspots of biodiversity, Threats and Conservation (In-situ, Ex-situ)

### Module 2: Natural Resources & Renewable Energy
- Renewable and Non-Renewable Resources: Forest, Water, Mineral, Food, Energy, and Land resources
- Over-exploitation and Deforestation, Impact of large dams on forests and tribal people
- Water Resources: Floods, Drought, Conflicts over water (Inter-state river water sharing)
- Energy Resources: Growing energy needs, Fossil fuels (Coal, Petroleum, Natural Gas)
- Renewable Energy Sources: Solar energy, Wind energy, Hydroelectric, Biomass, Geothermal, Ocean energy

### Module 3: Environmental Pollution & Waste Management
- Air Pollution: Causes, Effects (Greenhouse effect, Global warming, Acid rain, Ozone depletion), Control measures
- Water Pollution: Sources, Water quality parameters (BOD, COD), Sewage treatment, Control technologies
- Soil Pollution and Noise Pollution: Sources and impacts
- Solid Waste Management: Municipal solid waste, Biomedical waste, Hazardous waste handling
- Electronic Waste (E-Waste): Sources, Environmental hazards, Recycling and disposal methodologies

### Module 4: Environmental Legislation & Climate Agreements
- Environmental Protection Act 1986, Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act 1981, Water Act 1974
- Forest Conservation Act, Wildlife Protection Act
- Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA): Process, Stages, Importance in project approvals
- Global Environmental Protocols: Kyoto Protocol, Montreal Protocol, Paris Climate Agreement (COP)
- Carbon Footprint, Carbon Credits, and Clean Development Mechanisms (CDM)

### Module 5: Human Population, Disaster Management & Ethics
- Human Population Growth, Population explosion, Environment and Human Health
- Disaster Management: Earthquakes, Cyclones, Floods, Landslides — preparedness and response
- Role of Information Technology in Environment and Human Health
- Environmental Ethics: Issues and possible solutions, Consumerism and waste products
- Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs): 17 UN Goals, Circular economy principles
