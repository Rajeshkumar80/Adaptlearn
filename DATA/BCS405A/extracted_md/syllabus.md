## 🎯 Course Objectives

1. Understand principles of mathematical logic, propositional connectives, and proof techniques.
2. Apply mathematical induction and counting techniques to solve combinatorial problems.
3. Analyze relations, properties of relations, partial orders, lattices, and functions.
4. Formulate and solve recurrence relations using generating functions and characteristic equations.
5. Apply graph theory principles and tree structures to model computer network and data problems.

---

## 📚 Module-Wise Syllabus

### Module 1: Fundamentals of Logic
- Propositions and Logical Connectives: Negation, Conjunction, Disjunction, Conditional, Biconditional
- Truth Tables, Tautology, Contradiction, Logical Equivalence, Laws of Logic
- Rules of Inference and Valid Arguments (Modus Ponens, Modus Tollens, Syllogisms)
- Predicates and Quantifiers: Universal and Existential Quantifiers, Negation of Quantified Statements
- Methods of Proof: Direct proof, Proof by contradiction, Proof by contrapositive

### Module 2: Properties of Integers & Principles of Counting
- Mathematical Induction: The Well-Ordering Principle, Principle of Mathematical Induction
- Fundamental Principles of Counting: Rule of Sum and Rule of Product
- Permutations and Combinations (with and without repetition)
- The Binomial Theorem, Combinatorial Identities
- The Pigeonhole Principle and its generalized forms

### Module 3: Relations and Functions
- Cartesian Products, Relations: Domain and Range, Representation using Matrices and Digraphs
- Properties of Relations: Reflexive, Symmetric, Anti-symmetric, Transitive
- Equivalence Relations and Partitions, Equivalence Classes
- Partial Order Relations: Posets, Hasse Diagrams, Maximal, Minimal, Greatest, and Least Elements
- Functions: Injective (One-to-One), Surjective (Onto), Bijective functions, Composition of Functions, Inverses

### Module 4: Recurrence Relations & Generating Functions
- Principles of Inclusion and Exclusion: Formula, Derangements, Rook Polynomials
- Recurrence Relations: First-Order Linear Recurrence Relations
- Second-Order Linear Homogeneous Recurrence Relations with Constant Coefficients
- Non-Homogeneous Recurrence Relations: Method of Undetermined Coefficients
- Introduction to Generating Functions

### Module 5: Graph Theory and Trees
- Definitions and Examples of Graphs: Subgraphs, Complements, Graph Isomorphism
- Degree of Vertices, Handshaking Lemma, Regular Graphs, Bipartite Graphs, Complete Graphs
- Eulerian Paths and Circuits, Hamiltonian Paths and Cycles
- Planar Graphs and Euler's Formula
- Trees: Definitions and Properties, Spanning Trees, Rooted Trees, Binary Trees
