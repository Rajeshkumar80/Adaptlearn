## 🎯 Course Objectives

1. Apply probability theory and distributions to solve engineering problems.
2. Analyze joint probability distributions and model processes using Markov chains.
3. Understand and apply principles of statistical inference and hypothesis testing.
4. Determine statistical significance using ANOVA testing.
5. Develop analytical skills through problem-based learning for real-life engineering situations.

---

## 📚 Module-Wise Syllabus

### Module 1: Probability Distributions
- Review of basic probability theory
- Random variables — discrete and continuous
- Probability mass function and probability density function
- Mathematical expectation, mean, and variance
- **Binomial Distribution** — derivation of mean and standard deviation
- **Poisson Distribution** — derivation of mean and standard deviation
- **Normal Distribution** and **Exponential Distribution**

### Module 2: Joint Probability Distribution & Markov Chain
- Joint probability distribution for two discrete random variables
- Expectation, covariance, and correlation
- Introduction to stochastic processes
- Probability vectors and stochastic matrices
- Regular stochastic matrices
- **Markov Chains** — higher transition probabilities
- Stationary distribution and absorbing states

### Module 3: Statistical Inferences
- Sampling distributions
- Estimation: Point and interval estimation
- Confidence intervals for mean, variance, and proportions
- Student's t-distribution and Chi-square distribution

### Module 4: Hypothesis Testing
- Null and alternative hypotheses
- Type I and Type II errors
- Tests for means, proportions, and variances
- Z-test, t-test, Chi-square test

### Module 5: Design of Experiments (ANOVA)
- One-way ANOVA — completely randomized design
- Two-way ANOVA — randomized block design
- Latin square design
- Determining statistical significance of inputs on system responses
