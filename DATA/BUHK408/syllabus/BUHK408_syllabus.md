## 🎯 Course Objectives

1. Help students recognize the essential complementarities between values and professional skills.
2. Facilitate self-exploration regarding basic human aspirations: happiness and prosperity.
3. Understand the harmony in the human being (between 'I' and the Body).
4. Appreciate harmony in family, society, nature, and the cosmic order.
5. Develop holistic perception and ethical competence in professional conduct.

---

## 📚 Module-Wise Syllabus

### Module 1: Introduction to Value Education & Self-Exploration
- Need, Basic Guidelines, Content and Process for Value Education
- Self-Exploration: Content and process, Natural Acceptance and Experiential Validation
- Continuous Happiness and Prosperity: Basic Human Aspirations
- Right Understanding, Relationship, and Physical Facilities (The basic priority for fulfillment of human aspirations)
- Method to fulfill the basic human aspirations: Understanding and Living in Harmony at all levels

### Module 2: Harmony in the Human Being
- Understanding Human Being as the Co-existence of the Sentient 'I' and the Material 'Body'
- Distinction between Needs of the Self ('I') and Needs of the 'Body'
- Activities of the Self ('I') and Activities of the 'Body'
- Harmony of 'I' with the Body: Sanyam (Self-regulation) and Swasthya (Health)
- Correct appraisal of Physical Needs, Meaning of Prosperity in detail

### Module 3: Harmony in the Family and Society
- Harmony in the Family — the basic unit of human interaction
- Values in Relationships: Trust (Vishwas) — the foundational value, Respect (Samman) as right evaluation
- Difference between Respect and Differentiation, The other 7 values in relationship (Affection, Care, Guidance, Reverence, Glory, Gratitude, Love)
- Harmony in Society: Undivided Society (Akhand Samaj), Universal Human Order (Sarvabhaum Vyawastha)
- Five dimensions of Human Order: Education-Right Living, Health-Self-regulation, Production-Work, Justice-Preservation, Exchange-Storage

### Module 4: Harmony in Nature and Existence
- Harmony in Nature: The four orders of nature (Material, Pranic/Plant, Animal, Human Order)
- Interconnectedness and Mutual Fulfillment (Parasparta and Paraspar Purakta) among the four orders
- Recyclability and Self-regulation in nature
- Existence as Co-existence (Sah-astitva) of all units in space
- Holistic perception of harmony at all levels of existence

### Module 5: Professional Ethics & Holistic Living
- Definitiveness of Ethical Human Conduct, Basis for Humanistic Education, Constitution, and Universal Order
- Competence in Professional Ethics: Clarity on holistic technologies, production systems, and management models
- Typical hurdles and transition strategies from the present state to Universal Human Order
- Vision for Holistic Technologies and Systems: Eco-friendly, human-friendly production and lifestyle
- Case studies: Critical analysis of prevalent unethical practices and remedial models

## ✅ Course Outcomes (COs)

| CO | Description |
|---|---|
| **CO1** | Articulate the process of self-exploration and recognize continuous happiness and prosperity as core human aspirations. |
| **CO2** | Distinguish between the needs and activities of the Self ('I') and the Body to maintain health and self-regulation. |
| **CO3** | Examine trust, respect, and values in interpersonal relationships to foster harmony in family and society. |
| **CO4** | Analyze interconnectedness, mutual fulfillment, and self-regulation across all four orders of nature. |
| **CO5** | Apply principles of ethical human conduct and sustainable technologies in engineering and professional careers. |
