## 🎯 Course Objectives

1. Understand the principles, types, and foundational process of scientific research.
2. Formulate research problems, define research questions, and design research methodologies.
3. Learn methods of data collection, sampling techniques, statistical data analysis, and hypothesis testing.
4. Master research paper writing, literature review, citation styles, plagiarism, and research ethics.
5. Understand Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) including Patents, Copyrights, Trademarks, and Trade Secrets.

---

## 📚 Module-Wise Syllabus

### Module 1: Research Methodology Fundamentals & Problem Definition
- Meaning, Objectives, and Motivations of Research; Types of Research: Descriptive vs Analytical, Applied vs Fundamental, Quantitative vs Qualitative, Conceptual vs Empirical
- Research Approaches and the Research Process: Step-by-step framework
- Defining the Research Problem: Selecting the problem, Necessity of defining the problem, Techniques involved in defining a problem
- Literature Review: Importance, Primary and secondary sources, Critical review techniques, Identifying research gaps
- Formulating Research Hypotheses and Research Questions

### Module 2: Research Design & Sampling Methods
- Research Design: Meaning, Need, and Features of a good research design
- Important Concepts relating to Research Design: Dependent/Independent variables, Extraneous variables, Control, Confounded relationship
- Types of Research Designs: Exploratory, Descriptive, and Diagnostic research designs, Experimental designs (Completely Randomized, Randomized Block, Latin Square)
- Sampling Design: Census vs Sample survey, Steps in sampling design, Criteria for selecting a sampling procedure
- Sampling Techniques: Probability sampling (Simple random, Systematic, Stratified, Cluster) and Non-probability sampling (Convenience, Judgment, Quota, Snowball)

### Module 3: Data Collection & Statistical Data Analysis
- Methods of Data Collection: Primary data collection (Observation method, Interview method, Questionnaires, Schedules)
- Secondary Data Collection: Sources, Criteria for evaluating secondary data (Reliability, Suitability, Adequacy)
- Data Processing: Editing, Coding, Classification, and Tabulation of data
- Data Analysis and Interpretation: Descriptive statistics (Mean, Median, Mode, Standard Deviation)
- Inferential Statistics: Testing of Hypotheses (Parametric vs Non-parametric tests, t-test, z-test, ANOVA, Chi-square test)
- Interpretation of Results, Drawing valid conclusions

### Module 4: Technical Writing, Citations & Research Ethics
- Structure of a Scientific Research Paper / Thesis: Title, Abstract, Keywords, Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results and Discussion, Conclusion
- Referencing and Citation Styles: APA, IEEE, Harvard, Chicago styles; Reference management tools (Zotero, Mendeley)
- Research Ethics and Scientific Misconduct: Fabrication, Falsification, and Plagiarism (FFP)
- Plagiarism: Definition, Types of plagiarism, Plagiarism detection software (Turnitin, Urkund), Permissible similarity index
- Peer Review Process: Single-blind, Double-blind, Open peer review; Journal indexing (Scopus, Web of Science, SCI, Impact Factor, h-index, i10-index)

### Module 5: Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)
- Introduction to Intellectual Property: Nature, Importance, and Economic significance of IP
- International IP Treaties: WIPO, TRIPS Agreement, Paris Convention, Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT)
- Patents: Patentable criteria (Novelty, Inventive step / Non-obviousness, Industrial applicability), Non-patentable subject matter
- Patent Filing Process: Provisional and Complete specification, Examination, Publication, Grant, Patent opposition, Patent infringement
- Copyrights: Works protected, Rights of authors, Fair use doctrine, Digital copyright issues
- Trademarks: Definition, Functions, Registration process, Infringement, Passing off
- Trade Secrets, Industrial Designs, Geographical Indications (GI), and Plant Variety protection
