## 🎯 Course Objectives

1. Understand the generation, classification, and characteristics of municipal solid waste in urban ecosystems.
2. Learn waste collection, segregation, and smart routing technologies using IoT and GIS.
3. Master waste processing technologies: composting, biomethanation, incineration, and pyrolysis.
4. Analyze sanitary landfill design, leachate management, and gas recovery systems.
5. Understand waste management policies, Solid Waste Management Rules 2016, and smart city integration.

---

## 📚 Module-Wise Syllabus

### Module 1: Solid Waste Fundamentals & Smart City Dynamics
- Definition, Sources, and Classification of Solid Waste: Municipal, Commercial, Industrial, Agricultural, Construction & Demolition
- Physical and Chemical Characteristics of Solid Waste: Proximate and Ultimate analysis, Calorific value
- Solid waste generation rates and factors affecting generation in smart cities
- Waste hierarchy: 3R / 5R principles (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, Recover, Redesign)
- Environmental and health impacts of unscientific waste dumping

### Module 2: Waste Segregation, Collection & Smart Logistics
- Source Segregation: Wet waste (biodegradable), Dry waste (recyclables), Domestic hazardous waste
- Collection Systems: Door-to-door collection, Community bin systems
- Smart Bin Technology: IoT-enabled level sensors, Solar-powered trash compactors, Real-time bin monitoring
- Transfer and Transport: Transfer stations, Waste compaction vehicles
- GIS and GPS-based route optimization for waste collection vehicles in smart cities

### Module 3: Biological Waste Treatment Technologies
- Biological Treatment of Organic Waste: Principles, Microorganisms involved
- Composting: Aerobic composting, Windrow composting, In-vessel composting, Factors affecting composting (C:N ratio, moisture, temperature, aeration)
- Vermicomposting: Species of earthworms, Process, Harvesting, Quality of vermicompost
- Biomethanation (Anaerobic Digestion): Stages of anaerobic digestion, Biogas plant designs, Utilization of biogas for energy generation
- Bio-methanation vs Composting comparative analysis

### Module 4: Thermal Conversion & Landfill Management
- Thermal Conversion Technologies: Incineration (Combustion parameters, Mass-burn vs Refuse-Derived Fuel RDF)
- Pyrolysis and Gasification: Principles, Products (Syngas, Bio-oil, Biochar), Energy recovery
- Sanitary Landfills: Site selection criteria, Components of a secured landfill
- Leachate Generation: Environmental risks, Leachate collection and treatment systems
- Landfill Gas (LFG) Generation: Composition, Methane capture, Extraction and energy recovery systems, Landfill capping and post-closure monitoring

### Module 5: Special Wastes, Regulatory Policies & Smart Governance
- Special Waste Streams: E-waste management, Biomedical waste, Plastic waste recycling, Hazardous waste
- Solid Waste Management Rules 2016: Key mandates for municipalities, waste generators, and bulk waste generators
- Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) guidelines
- Public-Private Partnerships (PPP) and Informal sector (Ragpickers) integration
- Smart City Waste Management Dashboards: Citizen grievance reporting apps (Swachhata app), Performance benchmarking and Swachh Survekshan metrics
