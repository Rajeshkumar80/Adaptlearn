# BCS501 — Software Engineering & Project Management

- **Semester**: 5th Semester B.E. (CSE)
- **Course Code**: BCS501
- **Credits**: 4 (3:0:2:0)
- **Total Teaching Hours**: 50 Hours (10 Hours per module)
- **Evaluation**: Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE): 50 Marks | Semester End Exam (SEE): 50 Marks

---

## Module 1: Software & Software Engineering Processes

### Topics Covered
- **Introduction**: The nature of software, unique characteristics of WebApps, software engineering definition, software process framework activities, software engineering practice, and software myths (management, customer, practitioner myths).
- **Process Models**: Generic process model, process assessment and improvement, prescriptive process models (Waterfall Model, Incremental Process Models, Evolutionary Process Models - Prototyping, Spiral Model, Concurrent Models).
- **Unified Process & Team Models**: The Unified Process (Inception, Elaboration, Construction, Transition phases), Personal and Team Process Models (PSP and TSP).

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: Roger S. Pressman, *Software Engineering: A Practitioner's Approach*, 7th/8th Edition.

---

## Module 2: Requirements Engineering & Analysis Modeling

### Topics Covered
- **Understanding Requirements**: Requirements engineering tasks (Inception, Elicitation, Elaboration, Negotiation, Specification, Validation, Management), establishing groundwork, eliciting requirements, developing Use Cases.
- **Requirements Modeling**: Requirements analysis scenarios, building the requirements model, Scenario-based modeling (UML Use Case diagrams, Activity diagrams).
- **Class-based & Behavioral Modeling**: Class-based modeling (Identifying analysis classes, CRC cards, Class diagrams), Data modeling concepts, Flow-oriented modeling (DFD), Behavioral modeling (State diagrams).

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: Roger S. Pressman, *Software Engineering: A Practitioner's Approach*, 7th/8th Edition.

---

## Module 3: Agile Development & Software Architecture

### Topics Covered
- **Agile Development**: What is Agility?, Agility and the cost of change, What is an Agile process?, Agile principles, Extreme Programming (XP) - values, process, industrial XP.
- **Other Agile Models**: Scrum, Dynamic Systems Development Method (DSDM), Agile Unified Process (AUP), Tool set for agile process.
- **Design Concepts & Architecture**: Software design quality concepts, Abstraction, Architecture, Patterns, Information Hiding, Functional Independence, Architectural Design & Taxonomy.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: Roger S. Pressman, *Software Engineering: A Practitioner's Approach*, 7th/8th Edition.

---

## Module 4: Software Project Management & Planning

### Topics Covered
- **Introduction to SPM**: Importance of SPM, contract management, activities covered by software project management, plans, methods, and methodologies, categorizing software projects, stakeholders.
- **Project Evaluation & Selection**: Setting objectives, business case, project success and failure, cost-benefit evaluation techniques (Net Profit, Payback Period, NPV, IRR), risk evaluation.
- **Step Wise Project Planning**: Steps 0 to 10 in SPM planning framework.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 2: Bob Hughes, Mike Cotterell, Rajib Mall, *Software Project Management*, 5th/6th Edition, McGraw-Hill.

---

## Module 5: Project Scheduling, Risk Management & Quality

### Topics Covered
- **Activity Planning & Scheduling**: Work breakdown structure (WBS), network planning models, PERT/CPM chart creation, identifying critical path, activity float/slack.
- **Risk Management**: Risk identification, risk assessment, risk planning, risk monitoring (RAM & RMMM plan).
- **Quality & Maintenance**: Software quality assurance (SQA), ISO 9000 standards, Capability Maturity Model Integration (CMMI), Software maintenance and re-engineering.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 2: Bob Hughes, Mike Cotterell, Rajib Mall, *Software Project Management*, 5th/6th Edition.
