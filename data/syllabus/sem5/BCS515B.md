# BCS515B — Artificial Intelligence (Professional Elective - I)

- **Semester**: 5th Semester B.E. (CSE)
- **Course Code**: BCS515B
- **Credits**: 3 (3:0:0:0)
- **Total Teaching Hours**: 40 Hours (8 Hours per module)
- **Evaluation**: Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE): 50 Marks | Semester End Exam (SEE): 50 Marks

---

## Module 1: Introduction to AI & Intelligent Agents

### Topics Covered
- **Introduction**: What is Artificial Intelligence?, Foundations of AI, History of AI, State of the Art.
- **Intelligent Agents**: Agents and Environments, Concept of Rationality (PEAS - Performance Measure, Environment, Actuators, Sensors specification), Nature of Environments (Fully vs Partially Observable, Single vs Multi-agent, Deterministic vs Stochastic, Episodic vs Sequential, Static vs Dynamic, Discrete vs Continuous, Known vs Unknown), Structure of Agents (Simple reflex agents, Model-based reflex agents, Goal-based agents, Utility-based agents, Learning agents).

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: Stuart J. Russell, Peter Norvig, *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, 3rd/4th Edition, Pearson.

---

## Module 2: Uninformed Search & Problem Solving

### Topics Covered
- **Problem Solving Agents**: Well-defined problems and solutions, Formulating problems, Example problems (8-Puzzle, 8-Queens, Vacuum World, Route Finding).
- **Uninformed Search Strategies**: Breadth-First Search (BFS), Depth-First Search (DFS), Uniform-Cost Search (Dijkstra variant), Depth-Limited Search, Iterative Deepening Depth-First Search (IDDFS), Bidirectional Search, Comparing search strategies (Time, Space, Completeness, Optimality).

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: Stuart J. Russell, Peter Norvig, *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, 3rd/4th Edition.

---

## Module 3: Informed Search & Propositional Logic

### Topics Covered
- **Informed (Heuristic) Search**: Greedy Best-First Search, A* Search (Admissible and Consistent heuristics, Proof of A* optimality), Memory-bounded heuristic search (SMA*), Local Search Algorithms (Hill-Climbing search, Simulated Annealing, Genetic Algorithms).
- **Logical Agents**: Knowledge-based agents, The Wumpus World environment, Logic syntax and semantics, Propositional Logic, Equivalence, Validity, Satisfiability, Forward and Backward Chaining in Propositional Logic.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: Stuart J. Russell, Peter Norvig, *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, 3rd/4th Edition.

---

## Module 4: First-Order Logic (FOL) & Inference

### Topics Covered
- **First-Order Logic (FOL)**: Representation revisited, Syntax and Semantics of FOL (Quantifiers: Universal \(\forall\) and Existential \(\exists\)), Assertions and Queries in FOL, Using FOL for Knowledge Engineering.
- **Inference in First-Order Logic**: Propositional vs First-Order Inference, Unification and Lifting, Forward Chaining in FOL, Backward Chaining in FOL.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: Stuart J. Russell, Peter Norvig, *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, 3rd/4th Edition.

---

## Module 5: Resolution in FOL & Classical Planning

### Topics Covered
- **Resolution in FOL**: Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF) conversion for FOL sentences, Resolution rule of inference, Resolution Refutation proofs.
- **Classical Planning**: Definition of Classical Planning (PDDL - Planning Domain Definition Language), State-Space Search for Planning (Forward State-space search, Backward Relevant-states search), Planning Graphs and GraphPlan algorithm.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: Stuart J. Russell, Peter Norvig, *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, 3rd/4th Edition.
