# BCS503 — Theory of Computation

- **Semester**: 5th Semester B.E. (CSE)
- **Course Code**: BCS503
- **Credits**: 4 (3:2:0:0)
- **Total Teaching Hours**: 50 Hours (10 Hours per module)
- **Evaluation**: Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE): 50 Marks | Semester End Exam (SEE): 50 Marks

---

## Module 1: Finite Automata & Regular Expressions

### Topics Covered
- **Central Concepts of Automata Theory**: Alphabet, Strings, Languages, Formal Proofs, Proof by Contradiction, Proof by Mathematical Induction.
- **Deterministic & Nondeterministic Finite Automata**: DFA formal definition, state transition diagrams and tables, NFA formal definition, Language accepted by DFA/NFA, Equivalence of NFA and DFA, Subset Construction algorithm.
- **NFA with Epsilon-Transitions (\(\epsilon\)-NFA)**: Extended transition function, \(\epsilon\)-closure, conversion of \(\epsilon\)-NFA to DFA.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: John E. Hopcroft, Rajeev Motwani, Jeffrey D. Ullman, *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation*, 3rd Edition, Pearson.

---

## Module 2: Regular Expressions, Pumping Lemma & DFA Minimization

### Topics Covered
- **Regular Expressions**: Operators (Union, Concatenation, Kleene Star), Equivalence of Regular Expressions and Finite Automata (Converting NFA to RE using State Elimination, Thompson's Construction of \(\epsilon\)-NFA from RE).
- **Properties of Regular Languages**: Pumping Lemma for Regular Languages, Applications to prove non-regularity of languages (e.g., \(L = \{a^n b^n \mid n \ge 0\}\)), Closure properties (Union, Intersection, Complement, Reversal, Homomorphism).
- **Equivalence & DFA Minimization**: Table Filling Algorithm (Myhill-Nerode theorem), Minimization of DFA states.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: John E. Hopcroft, Rajeev Motwani, Jeffrey D. Ullman, *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation*, 3rd Edition.

---

## Module 3: Context-Free Grammars (CFG) & Pushdown Automata (PDA)

### Topics Covered
- **Context-Free Grammars**: Formal definition of CFG (\(V, T, P, S\)), Derivations (Leftmost and Rightmost derivations), Parse Trees, Yield of a parse tree, Ambiguous Grammars and Ambiguity elimination.
- **Pushdown Automata (PDA)**: Definition of PDA (7-tuple), Instantaneous Descriptions (ID), Language accepted by PDA (Acceptance by Final State vs Acceptance by Empty Stack), Equivalence of Acceptance by Empty Stack and Final State, Equivalence of CFG and PDA (Constructing PDA from CFG and vice versa), Deterministic Pushdown Automata (DPDA).

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: John E. Hopcroft, Rajeev Motwani, Jeffrey D. Ullman, *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation*, 3rd Edition.

---

## Module 4: Normal Forms for CFG & Properties of CFLs

### Topics Covered
- **Grammar Simplification & Normal Forms**: Eliminating \(\epsilon\)-productions, Unit productions, and Useless symbols from CFG. Chomsky Normal Form (CNF) conversion, Greibach Normal Form (GNF) overview.
- **Properties of Context-Free Languages**: Pumping Lemma for Context-Free Languages (Ogden's Lemma), Applications to prove non-context-free languages (e.g., \(L = \{a^n b^n c^n \mid n \ge 0\}\)), Closure properties of CFLs (Union, Concatenation, Kleene Star; non-closure under Intersection and Complement).

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: John E. Hopcroft, Rajeev Motwani, Jeffrey D. Ullman, *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation*, 3rd Edition.

---

## Module 5: Turing Machines, Decidability & Complexity

### Topics Covered
- **Turing Machines (TM)**: Formal definition of TM (7-tuple), Instantaneous Descriptions, Language of TM (Recursively Enumerable vs Recursive languages), Design of Turing Machines for standard languages (e.g., \(a^n b^n\), Palindromes, Arithmetic operations).
- **TM Extensions & Undecidability**: Multi-tape Turing Machines, Nondeterministic Turing Machines (Equivalence with standard TM), Universal Turing Machine (UTM), Church-Turing Thesis, Undecidability, Halting Problem of Turing Machine, Post Correspondence Problem (PCP), Introduction to P and NP Complexity Classes.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: John E. Hopcroft, Rajeev Motwani, Jeffrey D. Ullman, *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation*, 3rd Edition.
