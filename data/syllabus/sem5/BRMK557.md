# BRMK557 — Research Methodology and IPR

- **Semester**: 5th Semester B.E. (Mandatory Core across branches)
- **Course Code**: BRMK557
- **Credits**: 3 (3:0:0:0)
- **Total Teaching Hours**: 40 Hours (8 Hours per module)
- **Evaluation**: Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE): 50 Marks | Semester End Exam (SEE): 50 Marks

---

## Module 1: Introduction to Research & Research Ethics

### Topics Covered
- **Engineering Research**: Meaning and objectives of engineering research, Motivation in research, Types of engineering research (Fundamental, Applied, Experimental, Theoretical), Identifying a research problem, Defining scope and objectives.
- **Research Ethics**: Ethics in engineering research practice, Types of research misconduct (Fabrication, Falsification, Plagiarism - FF&P), Plagiarism check tools (Turnitin, iThenticate), Authorship ethics and conflicts of interest.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: Dipankar Deb, Rajeeb Dey, Valentina E. Balas, *Engineering Research Methodology*, Springer.

---

## Module 2: Literature Review, Critical Reading & Citations

### Topics Covered
- **Literature Review & Synthesis**: Purpose of literature survey, Searching bibliographic databases (IEEE Xplore, Web of Science, Scopus, Google Scholar), Analyzing prior art.
- **Technical Reading & Attribution**: Critical reading strategies, Reading algorithm papers and mathematical formulas, Reference citation styles (IEEE format, APA format), Functions of citations, Citation index and h-index/i10-index metrics.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 1: Dipankar Deb, Rajeeb Dey, Valentina E. Balas, *Engineering Research Methodology*, Springer.

---

## Module 3: Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) & Patenting Process

### Topics Covered
- **IPR Fundamentals**: Role of Intellectual Property in economic development, Types of IP (Patents, Copyrights, Trademarks, Designs, GIs, Trade Secrets), Indian IP governance framework (CGPDTM).
- **Patents**: Conditions for patentability (Novelty, Non-obviousness/Inventive Step, Industrial Applicability), Non-patentable subject matter (Section 3 & 4 of Indian Patent Act), Patent drafting, Provisional vs Complete Specification, Prior Art Search, Patent Infringement.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 2: Prabuddha Ganguli, *Intellectual Property Rights: Unleashing the Knowledge Economy*, McGraw Hill.

---

## Module 4: Copyrights & Trademarks

### Topics Covered
- **Copyrights**: Copyright classes (Literary, Musical, Dramatic, Artistic, Sound recordings, Computer software), Author rights, Term of copyright, Infringement and Fair Use doctrine in educational research, Copyright registration in India.
- **Trademarks**: Trademark eligibility, Types of marks (Trade marks, Service marks, Collective marks), Trademark registration process, Madrid Protocol, Infringement and Passing Off.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 2: Prabuddha Ganguli, *Intellectual Property Rights: Unleashing the Knowledge Economy*, McGraw Hill.

---

## Module 5: Industrial Designs, Geographical Indications (GI) & Case Studies

### Topics Covered
- **Industrial Designs & GI**: Industrial Design registration process under Indian Designs Act 2000, Geographical Indications (GI) definition, eligibility, rights of GI holders, GI registration process.
- **IP Case Studies**: Case studies on revocation of Turmeric (Curcuma) Patent, Neem Patent revocation, Basmati Rice patent battle, and landmark IP litigation in India.

### Prescribed Textbooks
- Text Book 2: Prabuddha Ganguli, *Intellectual Property Rights: Unleashing the Knowledge Economy*, McGraw Hill.
