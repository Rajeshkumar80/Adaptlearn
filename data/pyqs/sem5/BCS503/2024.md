# BCS503 — Previous Year Question Paper (Jan/Feb 2024 Examination)

- **Course Code**: BCS503
- **Duration**: 3 Hours
- **Max Marks**: 100

---

## Module 1

### Question 1
- **Q1(a)** [Module 1 | 10 Marks | L3]: Construct a DFA accepting all binary strings over \(\Sigma = \{0, 1\}\) ending with `101` or `010`. Show state transition table and trace input string `110101`.
- **Q1(b)** [Module 1 | 10 Marks | L2]: Define \(\epsilon\)-closure of a state. Show steps to convert an \(\epsilon\)-NFA to DFA without \(\epsilon\)-transitions.

---

## Module 2

### Question 3
- **Q3(a)** [Module 2 | 10 Marks | L3]: Convert the Regular Expression \(r = (0 + 1)* 01\) to NFA using Thompson's Construction method, then to DFA.
- **Q3(b)** [Module 2 | 10 Marks | L2]: State closure properties of Regular Languages under Union, Intersection, Complement, Reversal, and Homomorphism with formal proofs.

---

## Module 3

### Question 5
- **Q5(a)** [Module 3 | 10 Marks | L3]: Construct a PDA for accepting language \(L = \{a^n b^{2n} \mid n \ge 1\}\) by final state. Trace stack operations for string `a b b`.
- **Q5(b)** [Module 3 | 10 Marks | L2]: Convert CFG \(S \to a S b \mid \epsilon\) into an equivalent Pushdown Automaton.

---

## Module 4

### Question 7
- **Q7(a)** [Module 4 | 10 Marks | L3]: Eliminate Useless symbols, \(\epsilon\)-productions, and Unit productions from the grammar:
  - \(S \to A a \mid B\)
  - \(A \to a \mid B c \mid \epsilon\)
  - \(B \to d \mid A A\)
- **Q7(b)** [Module 4 | 10 Marks | L2]: Discuss closure properties of Context-Free Languages. Prove that CFLs are closed under Union and Concatenation, but NOT under Intersection.

---

## Module 5

### Question 9
- **Q9(a)** [Module 5 | 10 Marks | L3]: Design a Turing Machine that computes proper subtraction \(m - n = \max(m-n, 0)\) for unary encoded integers \(0^m 1 0^n\).
- **Q9(b)** [Module 5 | 10 Marks | L2]: Explain Post Correspondence Problem (PCP) and Modified PCP with an example instance.
