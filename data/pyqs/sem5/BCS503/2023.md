## Module 1

### Question 1
- **Q1(a)** [Module 1 | 10 Marks | L3]: Convert the following \(\epsilon\)-NFA to an equivalent DFA using Subset Construction algorithm:
  - States \(Q = \{q_0, q_1, q_2\}\), Alphabet \(\Sigma = \{a, b\}\), Start state \(q_0\), Final state \(q_2\).
  - Transitions: \(\delta(q_0, \epsilon) = \{q_1\}\), \(\delta(q_1, a) = \{q_1\}\), \(\delta(q_1, \epsilon) = \{q_2\}\), \(\delta(q_2, b) = \{q_2\}\).
- **Q1(b)** [Module 1 | 10 Marks | L2]: Define DFA and NFA formally. Prove that if a language \(L\) is accepted by an NFA, there exists an equivalent DFA accepting \(L\).
